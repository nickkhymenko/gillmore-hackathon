# Commercial Model: car telematics, applied to pet insurance

**Pitch line:** "Black boxes changed car insurance for young drivers. Petra does the same for pets: careful owners pay less, insurers pay out less, and nobody is penalised for having an older or poorly pet."

Verify any market numbers with the research prompt (`docs/04`) before they go on a slide. Everything below is the mechanism, not sourced statistics.

## 1. What car telematics does, and the pet equivalent

| Car insurance telematics | How it works for cars | Petra for pets |
|---|---|---|
| **Pay How You Drive** (driving score) | Score from braking, speed, time of day; better score, lower renewal price | **Pay How You Care**: Care Score from hitting the pet's personal activity target, weekly check-ins, vaccinations and parasite control on time, dental and weight checks. Better score, lower premium |
| **Pay As You Drive** (per mile) | Low-mileage drivers pay less | **Flex cover**: core cover plus optional add-ons the owner switches on when needed (e.g. travel abroad, boarding, dog walker cover) |
| **Young driver black box policies** | Makes cover affordable for the highest-priced group | **First-pet and young-owner policies**: Gen Z and Millennial owners (69% say their pet is family) who skip insurance on price get an entry price that drops as they prove good care |
| **Bonus miles / rewards** | Extra free miles for good driving | **Bonus care**: free online vet consults, nurse checks, dental checks earned by the Care Score (cheap for insurer, prevents claims) |
| **Self-selection** | Careful drivers choose telematics, improving the risk pool | **Favourable selection**: engaged owners opt in, so the insurer attracts lower-risk, higher-retention customers. A core commercial argument |
| **Crash data and First Notice of Loss** | Box detects a crash, speeds up claims, cuts fraud | **Early warning and claims evidence**: activity dips trigger an early consult; the owner can choose to attach trend data to a claim, speeding it up and resolving disputes (e.g. showing when symptoms started) |
| **Stolen vehicle tracking** | GPS recovers stolen cars, reduces theft claims | **Lost and stolen pet tracking**: collar GPS cuts loss and theft claims and advertising and reward costs, which many pet policies cover. Dog theft is a live UK issue |
| **Parent / learner app** | Parents see the learner's driving | **Family and walker sharing**: household members, dog walkers and sitters log walks to the same pet; owners see walks happened |
| **Device options** | Fitted box, plug-in, then smartphone app | **Phone first** (free, today), **collar** (insurer-subsidised), **any Bluetooth sensor** later |
| **Renewal price** | Score applied at renewal | Discount applied monthly as a visible "savings meter", with the full tier locked in at renewal |

## 2. Lessons from car telematics (use these in Q&A)

1. **Penalties killed trust.** Curfews, surcharges and cancellations for bad scores caused complaints and bad press. **Petra is discount-only: never a surcharge, never a cancellation, never a new exclusion.**
2. **"Big Brother" fears limited uptake.** **Location never leaves the phone; the insurer sees only "target met: yes or no" and the tier.**
3. **Hardware was expensive to fit and support.** The market moved to smartphone apps. **Petra starts phone-first; the collar is optional.**
4. **Scores felt unfair and opaque.** **Petra's targets are personal to breed, age and health, explained in plain English, with Rest mode for injuries.**

## 4. Why insurers buy it (the commercial case on one slide)

1. **Acquisition:** a reason for price-sensitive young owners to choose this insurer (and to insure at all).
2. **Selection:** engaged owners are lower risk.
3. **Claims:** earlier, cheaper interventions; lower severity; fewer theft/loss payouts; less fraud.
4. **Retention:** a weekly touchpoint and a discount that grows with tenure.
5. **Data (aggregated, anonymised, consented):** better understanding of risk by breed and age over time.
6. **Distribution:** white-label inside the insurer's own app via Doctapet, no new app for the customer.

## 5. Fairness and regulation (UK)

- **FCA Consumer Duty:** fair value, clear communications, no foreseeable harm; discount-only design and plain-English scores support this.
- **UK GDPR:** location and owner data minimised (location stays on device), granular consent, right to erasure ("Delete all my data").
- **Discount, not discrimination:** personal targets mean senior, disabled and low-energy breeds can earn the full discount; Rest mode protects it during illness or injury.
- **Veterinary Surgeons Act 1966:** activity changes are "signs worth a vet's look", never a diagnosis.
