# Chosen: Problem Statement 1, "Prevention that pays"

**Brief:** Design a prevention product an insurer would offer inside its own app: regular health scans, online consultations and earlier care. Show how it works for all four parties: the pet parent, the vet, the insurer and the underwriter.

**Why this matters:** A product and business case we can take to insurers, with value for every party, not one at another's expense.

## Context (given)
- UK pet insurers paid a record £1.23bn in 2024 across 1.8 million claims; the average claim was £685.
- **Most claims start as a question an owner had days or weeks earlier.**
- Dogs and cats lead the market, but horses, rabbits and exotic pets are insured too. We may design for any of these animals.
- Pet parent: what makes a regular scan a habit rather than a chore?
- Vet: how does the practice benefit from earlier, regular care?
- Underwriter: can preventive health data improve how risk is priced, and where are the fairness and regulatory limits?

## What teams produce
1. The product from the perspective of all four parties.
2. The member journey, from the first health scan to a vet visit.
3. A business case covering claims, retention and costs avoided.
4. A pricing model, such as per policy, per member or per claim avoided.
5. The data an underwriter would need and the limits on how it could be used.

## Judged focus areas
Value for all four parties · Strength of the business case · Realism about regulation and data use · Clarity of the member journey.
